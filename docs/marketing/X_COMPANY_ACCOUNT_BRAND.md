## Posting Strategy for Company Account

- Post 2-3 times a week.
- Mix: user stories (40%) + product philosophy (25%) + culture/behind-scenes (20%) + engagement/Q&A (15%).
- Always link back to product when relevant, but don't make every post a sell.
- Share wins confidently. Celebrate customers (with permission).
- Engage with compliance, fintech, and startup communities.
- Retweet user stories and tag teams.
- Monthly: do a "state of TreY" thread - what we shipped, what we learned, what's next.

## Brand Voice

- Direct, honest, no corporate-speak.
- Data-driven when possible ("50-person team reduced misses from 3/quarter to 0").
- Specific over generic ("TreY reduces audit prep time by 80%" vs "TreY makes audits easier").
- User-centric always ("helps teams" not "our platform").
- India-first perspective (don't pretend to be SF startup).
- Anti-hype (no "revolutionary," no "game-changing," no AI buzzwords).
