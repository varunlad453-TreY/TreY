## 1. The Core Problem: Why Compliance is Broken
If your compliance process relies on spreadsheets and shoulder-taps, you don't have a compliance system—you have a panic-inducing bottleneck. Today, CISOs and Risk Managers spend 40% of their weeks chasing engineers for SOC 2 or ISO 27001 evidence. 

Audits devolve into chaos because ownership isn't enforced, deadlines are treated as suggestions, and spreadsheets can be edited silently. This ruins audit integrity and leaves organizations scrambling right before the auditor arrives. Compliance execution shouldn't rely on human memory.

    ## 2. Our Philosophy: Execution Over Interpretation
    We believe that surviving an audit requires bringing strict engineering discipline to compliance. To do this, we established three non-negotiable rules for the TreY platform:

    * **Single Ownership:** Every obligation must have exactly ONE owner. Shared responsibility means zero responsibility.
    * **Strict SLAs:** Deadlines must be immutable. If an SLA needs an extension, it requires a documented reason and log—not a silent cell update in Excel.
    * **Append-Only Audit Trails:** Keep a permanent, time-stamped log of every decision and evidence upload.

## 3. What We Have Built (Current Deployed Capabilities)
We have just hit code-complete on our MVP, deployed securely to the cloud. Here is what is actively running in production today:

* **Enforced Accountability Engine:** The system rejects any obligation creation unless it is firmly tied to a single owner and a rigid SLA.
* **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC):** Strict separation of duties between CISOs (global visibility), SecEng Managers (departmental visibility), and HR/Operators (isolated tasks).
* **100% Immutable Audit Trail:** Every status change, SLA extension, and file upload is permanently logged with the user's ID, timestamp, and action reason. The UI has completely disabled "delete" buttons for historical compliance records.
* **Enterprise-Grade Infrastructure:** We successfully modernized our database architecture to Neon Serverless PostgreSQL and deployed to Render, ensuring high availability and rigid data security.

## 4. What We Are Improving (Our Immediate Roadmap)
While our QA teams run functional tests to harden the system, our engineering roadmap for the next 60 days focuses on scale:
